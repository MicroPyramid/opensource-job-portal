# Mission, Vision & Values

## Vision

To create the most user-friendly and feature-rich job board platform that
scales from startups to enterprise-level recruitment needs.

## Aim

To democratize job searching and hiring by providing a powerful, free, and
open-source platform that connects talent with opportunities efficiently.

## Mission

PeelJobs exists to remove friction from hiring, on both sides of it.

### Accessibility

Hiring software should not be a budget line. PeelJobs is free and open source,
so a two-person startup and a thousand-person company run the same platform.
Anyone can self-host it, read how it works, and change it.

### Efficiency

Every step from posting a job to tracking an application should take as few
actions as it honestly can. Recruiters get applicant management that does not
require a spreadsheet; job seekers get search that understands what they typed.

### Empowerment

Job seekers own their profile and their data. Recruiters own their postings and
their pipeline. Neither is locked in — it is open source, and the data is in
your own database.

### Innovation

Prefer the simplest thing that works. PeelJobs dropped Elasticsearch for
PostgreSQL full-text search because at this scale the database was faster,
simpler, and removed an entire service from the deployment. Complexity has to
earn its place.

### Quality & Trust

Job seekers are trusting the platform with employment history, education and
contact details. That is not incidental data. Moderation exists so listings are
real, and security is treated as a first-order concern rather than a later
hardening pass.

## Core Values

**Equity** — the same tools for everyone, regardless of company size or budget.

**Transparency** — open source, in the open. How ranking works, what data is
stored, and what the platform emails you are all readable in the repository.

**User-Centricity** — built around what job seekers and recruiters actually do,
not around what is convenient to implement.

**Excellence** — root causes over workarounds. A hack that ships today is a bug
report next month.

**Community Impact** — connecting people to work is the point. Every
improvement in match quality is someone finding a job they would have missed.

## How We Deliver

### For companies

- Unlimited free job posting
- Applicant management with shortlisting and hiring workflows
- Company profiles that reach job seekers directly
- Team accounts with role-appropriate permissions
- Analytics on posting performance

### For job seekers

- Free search and applications, no paywall
- Full-text search with typo tolerance and stemming
- SEO-friendly browse pages by skill, city, industry and company
- Profiles carrying skills, education, employment history and resume
- Saved jobs and application tracking
- Email alerts for matching roles

## Our Commitment

PeelJobs stays free and open source under the MIT License. Anyone can use it,
host it, study it, modify it and redistribute it.
